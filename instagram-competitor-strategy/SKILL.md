# Instagram Competitor Strategy

Turn completed Instagram analysis reports into a comparative strategy for one client. Explain which formats, hooks, topics, CTAs, and audience angles appear strongest in the supplied evidence, how they fit the client's profile, and what to test over eight weeks.

## Inputs and intake

Accept competitor and client reports in Markdown, PDF, DOCX, CSV, XLSX, or pasted tables when readable. Ask for or recover:

- client name, niche, geography, offer, priority service, ideal audience, and eight-week goal;
- competitor names or handles and report dates;
- objective: reach, engagement, authority, leads, or mixed;
- desired number of recommendations and publishing capacity per week;
- constraints such as language, founder availability, production budget, brand rules, or formats the client will not publish.

Do not invent missing client context. Mark it Not provided and qualify dependent recommendations. Preserve conflicting observations and identify them for verification.

## Evidence handling

Read each report's methodology and limitations first. Prefer row-level Reel evidence with visible views or plays, engagement metrics, dates, format, hook, CTA, and evidence status. Follower count, public likes alone, polish, or a competitor's claim of virality do not prove conversion.

Normalize synonymous labels without erasing original wording. Keep competitor, Reel URL, date, and source report attached to every pattern. Exclude unavailable rows, carousels, static posts, Stories, Lives, and unverified estimates from performance comparisons. If public views are absent, do not make view-tier or virality claims; use clearly evidenced qualitative patterns only.

Use relative evidence: High-view means strongest visible-view band within the supplied sample; High-engagement requires comparable metrics and a defined denominator; Promising pattern requires two qualifying examples or one exceptional example labeled as a hypothesis; Unproven means thin or incomparable evidence. Never promise virality, growth, leads, or revenue. Convert uncertainty into a test with a pass or fail signal.

## Comparative analysis

Build an evidence matrix before recommendations. Compare performance signals; creative structure; audience and intent; and client fit with the client's offer, geography, positioning, proof, resources, and content gaps.

Identify cross-competitor patterns, competitor-specific advantages, and client-owned strengths. Distinguish observed pattern, inference, and test recommendation. Do not copy competitor wording, copyrighted creative, personal stories, or brand identity; adapt the mechanism to the client's expertise and audience.

Read [references/strategy-framework.md](references/strategy-framework.md) before synthesizing reports.

## Eight-week strategy

Produce a practical eight-week plan that sequences learning before scaling. Each week includes an objective, content tests, format, hook, topic, CTA, cadence, production or repurposing notes, and measurable checkpoint. Stay within stated capacity; if missing, label the assumption.

Include 3-5 strategic pillars; a test backlog ranked by evidence strength, client fit, learning value, and production effort; an executable content matrix; measurement separating reach, engagement, profile actions, qualified inquiries, and booked calls; stop, continue, and iterate rules with reviews at weeks 2, 4, 6, and 8; and a paid-promotion readiness check. Do not recommend ads until the organic profile, CTA, inquiry path, and tracking are sufficiently clear.

Every recommendation includes source competitor or report, source Reel URL when available, evidence level, observed pattern, client adaptation, CTA, test metric, and a Do not copy constraint. Prioritize recommendations rather than producing an undifferentiated idea list.

## Deliverable and limits

Default output is one client-ready Markdown strategy report with evidence matrix, prioritized recommendations, eight-week plan, measurement framework, assumptions, conflicts, and limitations. If a spreadsheet is requested, create a separate sortable strategy workbook. Do not claim to have inspected accounts, private analytics, or Reels beyond what supplied reports document. Preserve source links and report dates.
